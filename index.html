Empathic Art v2 · dev previews

Engine test routes.

Standalone in-browser tests for the emotional-signal engines behind Empathic Art v2. Everything runs on your device — nothing is uploaded. Open these on an iPhone with Safari.

Engine 01 · confirmed
Human — face & emotion
Vladimir Mandic's Human library. Face box, 7-emotion classifier, valence/arousal mapping, EMA smoothing. iPhone Safari operating point: 4.75 fps sustained at 172 ms per inference.
Engine 02 · full test
VitalLens — pulse & breathing
rPPG heart rate from face pixels. Confidence-gated 5s median smoothing, personal resting-HR baseline lock at 60s, 60-second sparkline, stability meter, method picker (pos / chrom / g).
Engine 03 · new
Fusion — Human + VitalLens
Both engines on one shared camera stream. Face-based valence/arousal fuses with heart-rate arousal to a single Circumplex point (arousal = 0.6·humanA + 0.4·clip((hr−baseline)/15); valence = humanV). Live 2D plot with 8s trail.
Engine 02 · smoke test
VitalLens — raw preflight
Instrumented catch-all listener that first confirmed VitalLens works on iPhone Safari. Kept around for debugging the library itself.
